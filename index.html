FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting our clinic.

How do I book an appointment?
Fill in the appointment form on this page with your name, email, and phone number. Our front desk will contact you to confirm a suitable time.
Do I need to visit for a check-up if my teeth feel fine?
Yes. Regular check-ups can identify early signs of decay or gum problems before they cause discomfort, and they help keep your teeth in good condition.
Do you treat children?
Yes. We offer family and children's dental care, including routine check-ups and age-appropriate guidance for oral hygiene.
Will a filling or extraction be painful?
Your dentist will explain the procedure, the options available, and the aftercare before any treatment begins, so you can make an informed decision and feel comfortable with the plan.
How often should I get a professional cleaning?
Professional cleaning removes plaque and hardened tartar that brushing cannot fully clear. Your dentist can recommend a schedule based on your oral health.
Can I ask about cosmetic options?
Yes. A cosmetic dental consultation lets you discuss the appearance of your teeth and explore suitable options with our team.
What if I have a dental concern and don't know who to call?
Contact our front desk directly for quick support or guidance on scheduling. You can also use the appointment form below.
